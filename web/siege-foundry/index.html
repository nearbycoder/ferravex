How to play

Focus the range to use the keyboard. With default bindings, Enter or Space starts; arrows or W A S D aim; Space fires; Escape pauses or resumes; R rebuilds. Launch power is fixed at 28 metres per second: change the angle to change the arc.

Saved or remapped controls take precedence over these defaults. Open Controls with the button above or F2 while the range is focused to inspect or change bindings. The editor calls starting “Play / confirm”, rebuilding “Restart”, and pausing “Pause / menu”.

Start or retry
Enter or Space. Starting or retrying after a result does not fire a shot. Release, then press Fire again to shoot.
Aim left or right
Left / Right arrows or A / D change the cannon heading.
Raise or lower the barrel
Up / Down arrows or W / S. The dotted line previews the arc.
Fire
Space or the in-game Fire button launches one cannonball. Wait for the 0.8-second reload, then press again; a press during reload is discarded.
Pause or resume
Escape or the in-game Pause / Resume button. Opening Controls or Details, or leaving the browser window, suspends this single-player range. Closing the panel or returning to the window resumes only if you have not manually paused.
Rebuild
R or the in-game Rebuild button restores the towers, original aim and all eight shots.

With a mouse, hold the left button and drag in the scene to aim; use Fire to shoot. The last cannonball has six seconds to settle before a loss. The in-game buttons provide Start, Again or Retry as appropriate.

Inside Controls: use Tab, Shift+Tab or Up / Down to select an item, and Enter to activate it. Escape cancels a binding capture; Escape again closes the editor. F2 also closes it. Close Controls before using Tab to leave the range.

Page navigation: Tab or Shift+Tab moves between the range and page controls when Controls is closed. Keep Tab for page navigation when remapping game actions. Pause before reading this guide; moving focus within the page does not pause the range.

Player tools: open Details → Player tools for accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Escape closes Details and returns focus to the range. In fullscreen, Escape may exit fullscreen before reaching the game.
