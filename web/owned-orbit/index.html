How to play · keyboard and remapping

Default keys: A / Left arrow and D / Right arrow move; Space or Enter launches or retries; Escape pauses or resumes; R restarts. Open Controls or press F2 in the playfield to view or remap current bindings.

Pause before reading help. Shift + Tab from the playfield moves to How to play; use Shift + Tab again to reach the toolbar buttons. Moving focus or opening this help does not pause the game.

Default controls

Saved remaps take precedence over these defaults. The Controls panel shows your current bindings.

Move the paddle
Hold A or Left arrow to move left; D or Right arrow to move right.
Launch / retry
Press Space or Enter. After a win or loss, retry readies a fresh run; release, then press again to launch.
Pause / resume
Press Escape in the playfield or use Pause / Resume below it. Release held controls before resuming.
Restart
R or Restart clears the score, restores three lives and readies a fresh ball. Use a separate press to launch.

Menus and keyboard focus

Use Controls or F2 for the in-canvas remapping panel. Tab or Up / Down arrow selects an entry; Enter activates it. Escape cancels a binding capture; press it again to close Controls, or choose Done. Closing Controls preserves any existing pause.

Shift + Tab leaves the canvas for this help heading even while Controls is open, without closing that panel. Enter or Space expands the help; Escape closes this help and keeps focus on its heading. Use Shift + Tab from the heading to reach the toolbar, then Tab / Shift + Tab to navigate the page.

Open Details → Player tools for accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Escape or Done closes Details and returns focus to the canvas. Switching away from the browser pauses play; returning requires an explicit resume.

Serve and return

Wait before launch to charge your serve. Full charge pierces reinforced panels until the first paddle return. Each launch gets one opening ball save. Paddle edges angle your return. Catch capsules: turquoise widens the paddle, violet splits off two free bonus balls, and green makes the next three returns stick until you launch again. Chain panel hits without touching the paddle for volley callouts, clear a sector without losing a ball for a flawless bonus, and keep an eye on the gap above the panels. Your best score is kept on this device.

With default pointer controls, click or tap the playfield to launch or retry; hold and drag to steer. Release before launching again. Controls also lists controller bindings and touch settings.
