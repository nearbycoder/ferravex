Pocket Rally

Pocket Rally is a graphical rally time trial. JavaScript and WebGL2 are required to play. Open How to race below for the race objective and keyboard help.

Loading Pocket Rally — three laps, sixteen gates per lap…

A top-down rally time trial: clear sixteen marked gates in order on each of three laps. The race starts automatically with a 3, 2, 1, GO countdown. Grass slows the car; a translucent ghost shows your best lap, and green or red splits show whether you are ahead of it. Hold the handbrake through bends to drift for style points, chain drifts for a multiplier, keep laps clean for a bonus and finish under 43 seconds for gold. The farm ducks look bored.

Focus the game canvas to drive. Tab leaves the race canvas; Shift+Tab leaves it even while Controls is open. Pause before reading this guide; opening the guide does not pause the race.

The keys below are defaults. Saved bindings may differ. Open Controls (F2 while the canvas is focused) to view or change your current keyboard and gamepad bindings.

Controls, menus and focus

In Controls, use arrow keys or Tab to choose an item and Enter to select it. Escape cancels a pending rebind first; otherwise it closes Controls. F2 also toggles Controls.

Details contains presentation settings and Player tools, including text size, contrast and reduced motion. Done or Escape closes Details and returns focus to the canvas. The Sound button enables game audio.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between page buttons, this help button and the game. Page buttons use their normal Enter and Space actions. The All games link returns to the local game catalog.
