Picnic Panic: how to play

You are a small, very determined raccoon at the annual village picnic. Five picnickers go about their afternoon in a sunny park; each has a vision cone that turns yellow with a question mark when they notice something and red with an exclamation mark when they give chase.

Cross off the hand-written to-do list in the top-left corner: steal a whole pie and carry it to your den by the south-west hedge, swap the prize marrow for a pinecone, walk off with the band's sheet music, sled a picnic blanket down the hill on the tea tray, ring the bell by the gate, get someone to sit on a whoopee cushion, and launch a watermelon off the seesaw. Finishing all seven reveals a secret finale; then escape home. Hide in bushes, under tablecloths or in the picnic basket to break line of sight. If a grown-up catches you, they carry you to your newest burrow: press jump six times to wriggle free. Bottle caps and hidden bonus tasks unlock hats, and your best day is saved on this device with a speedrun ghost.

Focus the game to use its controls. Pause before leaving the game, then use Shift + Tab to move to the toolbar. From there, use How to play to reach these instructions. Controls shows your current bindings.

Default keyboard and mouse controls

These are defaults. Saved bindings may differ: open Controls or press F2 in the game to inspect or remap them.

Move and sneak
W A S D or the arrow keys. A gentle half press on a stick sneaks, which shrinks every vision cone.
Camera
Drag with the left mouse button, or hold Z / X. The mouse wheel zooms.
Jump
Space. Jump repeatedly to wriggle free when carried.
Grab, drop, place or use
E. Items are set on whatever surface is in front of you. Hold E at the bell rope (and for the finale).
Throw
F or the right mouse button.
Chitter
C makes a noise that lures nearby picnickers to investigate.
Belly-slide
Shift while running; in mid-air it becomes a belly-flop, handy on the seesaw.
To-do list and pause
N shows or hides the list. Escape pauses; the pause menu restarts the day or saves and leaves.

Menus, touch and gamepad

In the game menu, use Tab or the arrow keys to select a button and Enter to choose it. Choose Start the day to begin, or Continue to resume a saved to-do list. Wardrobe lists hats and how to unlock them.

On touchscreens, drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to turn the camera, and use the Jump, Grab, Throw, Chitter and Slide buttons. Tap the list to open it and the pause button to pause. On a gamepad, use the left stick to move, the right stick for the camera, A to jump, X to grab, B to throw, Y to chitter, RB to slide, LB for the list and Start to pause.

Opening Controls or switching away from the browser window or tab pauses the day. Closing Controls does not resume it; choose Resume. Reduced motion and reduced flash preferences calm camera shake, swaying and flashes. Vision cones and the best-run ghost can be turned off in the pause menu.

Use Details → Player tools for presentation preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Enable audio with Sound in the toolbar.
