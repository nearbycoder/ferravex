How to use the lab · default controls

An interactive 3D loading yard: stack crates, drop balls and inspect their collisions. The simulation starts automatically at 120 Hz; there is no start screen or win condition. Pause to inspect the scene or advance one physics tick at a time.

Click the yard or use Tab to focus the canvas before using game keys. With Controls closed, Tab and Shift+Tab move between the canvas and page controls. Tab is reserved for page navigation here; use another key for gameplay bindings. Open Controls with its button or F2 while the canvas is focused.

The bindings below are defaults. Saved or remapped controls can differ. Open Controls to view or change your current bindings; its action names are listed below. In Controls, use Tab or arrows to choose an item and Enter to activate it. Escape cancels a binding capture; otherwise Escape or F2 closes Controls. Close Controls before tabbing back to the page.

Orbit the camera
Hold the left mouse button and drag in the scene, or use W A S D / arrow keys (Left, Right, Forward / up, Back / down). The camera also works while manually paused.
Zoom
Scroll the mouse wheel over the scene (Zoom). Gamepad triggers control Zoom stick triggers.
Drop and select a shape
1 drops a crate (Drop crate); 2 drops a ball (Drop ball). Each selects that shape for repeat drops.
Repeat the selected drop
Enter (Drop selected shape). Drops work while paused and stay frozen until stepping or resuming.
Pause / resume
Space (Pause / menu). Escape is not the default pause key for this lab.
Step one tick
N (Step once while paused) advances 1/120 second and stays paused. Pause first.
Restore the camera
C (Reset orbit view) restores the view without resetting physics.
Reset the yard
R (Restart) rebuilds the yard, restores the camera and resumes the simulation.
Push nearby bodies
F (Impulse pulse) applies a radial kick while running, with a short cooldown.

On narrow screens, use Scene / Lab actions / Guide above the yard, or drag the right-hand Scroll page strip to move the page without orbiting. The buttons drawn along the bottom of the canvas also provide crate, ball, drop, pulse, pause/resume, step, view and reset actions. Their keyboard equivalents are listed above.

The Controls panel temporarily gates simulation and input. Returning from Controls or switching away from the browser preserves a manual pause. Details → Player tools contains presentation and accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Escape or Done closes Details and returns focus to the canvas; fullscreen may use Escape to exit first.
