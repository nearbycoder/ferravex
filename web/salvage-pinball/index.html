Salvage Pinball

Ferravex · All games

A salvage-yard pinball table. Keep three balls in play and hit the bumpers to score.

Default keys: A / Left arrow and D / Right arrow hold the flippers; Space starts and launches; Escape pauses or resumes. Release before each serve. Open Controls or press F2 in the table to view or remap your current bindings.

Pause before reading help. Shift + Tab from the table moves to How to play; use Tab to reach page controls. This focus shortcut does not pause the game.

Salvage Pinball requires JavaScript, WebAssembly and WebGL2. Keyboard instructions are available below in How to play.

How to play · keyboard and remapping

Default controls

These are the starting bindings. Saved remaps take precedence; the Controls panel shows your current bindings.

Left / right flipper
Hold A or Left arrow / D or Right arrow.
Start and launch
Press Space. Release, then press again for each serve, including after a ball save.
Start without launching
Press Enter at the ready screen, then use your launch binding.
Pause / resume
Press Escape while in the table. Release held controls before resuming.
Restart / retry
R starts a fresh three-ball game and clears the score. Enter also retries after game-over. Use a separate press to launch.

Menus and page navigation

Controls or F2 opens the in-canvas remapping panel. Use Tab or Up / Down arrow to select an entry and Enter to activate it. Escape cancels a binding capture; press it again to close Controls, or choose Done. Closing Controls returns to the table and preserves any existing pause.

Shift + Tab leaves the canvas for this help heading, even while Controls is open. It leaves the panel state unchanged. Press Enter or Space to expand this help. Continue with Tab for page links and buttons; Shift + Tab from this heading returns to the canvas.

Open Details → Player tools for accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Escape or Done closes Details and returns focus to the canvas.

Scoring and ball saves

Each bumper hit scores 100 points. The first launch of each life lights a five-second save for one early drain. A saved ball needs a new launch. Later drains consume a ball; after three unsaved drains, retry for a fresh game. Score, ball count and table status are drawn in the canvas.

With the default pointer controls, click or tap the table to launch; hold its lower left or right half to flip. Controls also lists controller bindings and touch settings. Keyboard help does not require touch input.
