Neon Swarm: how to play

A 2D arena survival shooter. Clear three waves of drones using cover, aimed shots and dashes. Amber rings and exclamation marks warn of melee attacks; move out of range before they strike.

Goals: chain kills for up to five times the points, dash through attacks for style, grab the green Repair and blue Shield supplies, and topple the armored Overseer that closes wave three. Every run ends with a rank, your personal best and a next goal. Rumor has it the wall lamps like being shot.

Focus the game to use its controls. Pause before leaving the arena, then use Shift + Tab to move to the toolbar. From there, use How to play to reach these instructions. Controls shows your current bindings.

Default keyboard and mouse controls

These are defaults. Saved bindings may differ: open Controls or press F2 in the game to inspect or remap them. Dash is listed as Boost / dash.

Move
W A S D or the arrow keys.
Aim and fire
Move the mouse to aim; hold the left mouse button to fire.
Dash
Press Space or either Shift key.
Pause or resume
Escape (the Pause / menu action).
Restart
R starts a fresh run.

Menus and returning to play

In the game menu, use Tab or the up/down arrows to select a button. The default Play / confirm binding is Enter: select Start, Resume, or Play again. You can also click those buttons.

In Controls, use Tab or the up/down arrows to navigate and Enter to select. Escape cancels a pending rebind; otherwise it closes the panel. F2 also closes Controls. Closing Controls does not resume a paused run.

Opening Controls or switching away from the browser window or tab pauses a manual run. On return, release held gameplay buttons, focus the game and choose Resume. Escape may first leave fullscreen or close a player panel.

Use Details → Player tools for presentation preferences, screenshots and settings backups. Enable audio with Sound in the toolbar.
