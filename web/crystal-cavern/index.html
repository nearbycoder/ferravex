Crystal Cavern

Crystal Cavern is a 3D exploration puzzle. The game needs JavaScript, WebAssembly and WebGL2. The expedition guide below explains its objective and keyboard controls.

Expedition guide

Explore a 3D cavern, recover three crystals, awaken altar I then altar II, and walk through the opened seal into daylight. Interact with the highlighted nearby object; aiming directly at it is unnecessary.

Focus the game canvas to play. With default bindings, press Enter to begin, resume or explore again, and Escape to pause or resume. Tab and Shift + Tab move between the game and page controls. Pause before reading the guide.

In Controls, use Up / Down and Enter to select a binding. Escape cancels an active remap; otherwise it closes Controls. F2 also closes Controls. Closing Controls preserves any manual pause.

Switching away from the browser or opening Controls pauses a normal expedition. Returning restores play unless you paused it manually. Use your current pause or confirm binding to resume.
