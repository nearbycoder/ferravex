Farming and village management

Hearthharvest

Complete three orders and finish day 14 with at least 80 coins.

Controls

Arrows Select | Act Work/Buy | X Crop/Sell | C Sleep | 1-5 Panels.

Act means Space or Enter. Touch the canvas to show the on-screen controls. Choose Play to start. H or Guide opens the rules.

Pause and restart

P or Pause opens the menu. Resume continues; New campaign starts over. F2 or Controls changes bindings. Sound is in the toolbar.

Completed scores are saved on this device. Campaigns in progress are not saved.
