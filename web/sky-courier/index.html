Flight guide

Fly through five amber gates in order, then make a low pass over the delivery cross at 18 metres per second or slower (about 35 knots). Deliver within 90 seconds. Three impacts end the flight; each impact also costs five seconds. Customers tip for style: thread each ring near its centre, keep a clean streak to multiply tips up to five times, and finish with a slow, centred drop. Three golden rings hide off the route for curious pilots. Your best delivered tips are kept on this device when browser storage is available.

Saved or remapped controls take precedence. The keys below are defaults. Open Controls or press F2 while focused on the game to inspect or change your current bindings. Changes save on this device when browser storage is available. Details → Player tools contains presentation and accessibility settings.

Default keyboard controls

Start / resume / fly again
Enter, or select the on-screen flight button.
Turn and bank
A / D or Left / Right arrows.
Climb / dive
W / S or Up / Down arrows.
Boost / slow flight
Space boosts; either Shift key slows for delivery.
Pause / resume
Escape during flight or a manual pause.
Restart flight
R restarts immediately, including from pause.

Release held flight controls after starting or retrying, then press them again. The briefing and pause screens freeze the delivery timer.

On short screens, use Panel top and Panel bottom to reach both ends of the flight panel without dragging on the game. The page outside the game also scrolls. Pause before browsing the page. With Controls closed, Tab and Shift + Tab move between the game and page controls. Inside Controls, use Tab or arrow keys and Enter; Escape cancels a binding capture first, then closes Controls. Escape also closes Details. Fullscreen may use the first Escape to exit fullscreen.
