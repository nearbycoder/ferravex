Marble Run

How to play
Marble Run needs a browser with canvas and WebGL2 support. Game instructions follow below.
Loading original Rust engine…

Choose one of four courses. After the countdown, roll the marble, collect every crystal and reach the finish arch.

With the game focused, press Shift+Tab to reach the page controls. Outside the game, Tab moves between page controls and instructions. Pause first if you want the trial to wait.

How to play — default keys and remapping

Focus the game by clicking it or using Tab from the page controls. In the course picker, use arrow keys or Tab to move the selection and Enter to choose a course. The countdown starts when you choose.

Gold means beating the course target with no falls; the chip under the clock shows which medal is still within reach and for how long. Every course also hides one optional moonstone off the racing line. Your results card shows your medal, your best time, a rank title and what to aim for next.

Default gameplay keys

Saved or remapped controls can differ from these defaults. Open Controls above, or press F2 with the game focused, to inspect your current bindings and remap actions. Changes save on this device when storage is available.

Roll
WASD or arrow keys, relative to the camera.
Brake
Hold Space.
Course overview
Hold Tab during play.
Choose course
C opens or closes the course picker. Choose the current course’s Resume card to keep an unfinished run.
Recover
V returns to the latest checkpoint after GO while playing. Crystals are kept; recovery counts as a fall.
Restart
R starts the current trial again, including the countdown.
Pause / resume
Escape during play. Use your Pause / menu binding if remapped.

Menus and returning to play

In Controls, use Up/Down or Tab and Enter. Escape cancels a binding capture; otherwise Escape or F2 closes Controls. In this browser host, Shift+Tab leaves the canvas; use Up to move back in Controls.

The course picker closes with your Choose course binding (default C), or the current course’s Resume card. Closing it keeps any separate manual pause. Countdown and trial time stop while paused; resume with your Pause / menu binding when needed.

Details → Player tools contains the shared accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups. In Details, Done or Escape closes the dialog and returns focus to the game.
