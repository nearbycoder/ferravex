How to play · rules, course and controls

Putt into all twelve cups using as few strokes as possible. Both views share exactly the same authored course and golf rules. Every hole has its own obstacle: stone banks, sand, water, a raised green, a rotating windmill or sliding gates.

Focus the canvas to play. Use Game menu for Controls, Settings, the other view and fullscreen. In fullscreen the course fills the viewport; Shift+Tab reaches the game menu.

Your first putt

Choose Start round, then drag from the ball toward the target and release to putt. Farther means more power. A tap changes your aim without shooting. You can also use the on-screen angle and power buttons, then PUTT.

Default keyboard: Left/Right (or A/D) aim, Up/Down (or W/S) change power, Space putts, P or Escape pauses, C opens the scorecard, R returns the ball for one penalty stroke. Enter activates the focused game menu item. Saved bindings can differ: use Controls / F2 for current bindings, controller support and remapping.

Read the green

Sand increases rolling resistance. Water adds one penalty and returns the ball to its last safe lie. The raised green changes the ball's speed. Solid walls bounce the ball; copper barriers move on fixed ticks. The cup only captures a sufficiently slow ball. The dotted line shows your initial aim, not a guaranteed path.

A hole ends in the cup or at the twelve-stroke pickup limit. Pause offers Take 12 and New round; the scorecard records pickup scores. Practice selects any hole and never changes a full-round best. Records stay on this device; storage failures leave the session playable.

Controls and Details suspend the local game. Reduced motion and reduced flash are shared player preferences; moving course obstacles remain visible because they are part of the rules. No camera shake or flashing effects are required to play.
