Lab instructions

The sandbox starts running immediately: balls, crates and jointed mechanisms move inside fixed boundaries. Place shapes, pause to inspect them, or advance one 120 Hz tick at a time. There is no win condition. The scene and its changing diagnostics are drawn visually in the canvas.

Focus the canvas to use game keys. With Controls closed, Tab or Shift + Tab leaves the canvas for page navigation. Use F2 or the Controls button to view bindings. In Controls, Tab or the up/down arrows select items and Enter activates them. Escape cancels a binding capture first; press it again to close Controls. Escape also closes Details. Use the pause action to freeze the lab.

Default keyboard and mouse controls

These are the default bindings. Saved or remapped controls may differ; Controls shows your current bindings and lets you change them. Tab is reserved for page navigation while Controls is closed.

W A S D / arrow keys
Move the virtual cursor.
1 / 2
Select a ball / crate.
Left click / Enter
Place the selected shape at the cursor. Enter drops above center when the cursor is outside the scene.
Right click
Place a crate without changing the selected shape.
Space
Pause or resume. Shapes placed while paused stay still.
N
Advance one tick while paused. Each press steps once; holding does not repeat.
F
Pulse nearby bodies while running. Wait for the pulse cooldown before pulsing again.
I
Inspect the body at the cursor without waking it. A miss clears body selection.
R
Restore the original scene, keeping the selected shape and pause state.

The lab has a 240-body limit. The canvas shows awake/asleep counts, last-tick contacts, bounded cursor-query hits and selected-body diagnostics, along with the tick and pulse readiness. A successful drop selects the new body. Reset clears body inspection while retaining the drop shape and pause state. The BALL, CRATE, DROP, PULSE, PAUSE / RESUME, STEP and RESET buttons also operate the lab. On narrow screens, the diagnostics wrap at a fixed readable size. Use the Scene, Lab controls and Guide links to move around the taller canvas; the section-link bar also provides a page scrolling surface.

Opening Controls or Details, or switching away from the browser, suspends this local lab. Closing the panel or returning preserves a manually paused scene. Details → Player tools contains presentation and accessibility preferences, screenshots and settings backups.
