How to play

Race a hovercraft through a 3D desert circuit: three laps of fourteen ordered gates, for 42 gate passes. Follow the teal next gate and cross it forwards inside the opening. Thread the center of each gate to refill boost and build a streak multiplier, then chase gold: beat Dusty the demo bot's 29.92 seconds.

The countdown starts when the game loads. Click the canvas to drive. From the canvas, Ctrl + F6 moves focus to All Ferravex games; then Tab moves through page controls. Use the Return to game link below to focus the canvas again. Moving to this guide does not pause the race; use your Pause action first.

Keyboard defaults are listed below. Saved or remapped bindings may differ. Open Controls or press F2 in the canvas to inspect and change your current bindings. The Controls list is the source for your active controls.

Race and menu tips

Threaded gates refill 10% boost, rising to 18% on a five-gate streak; clipping a gate, hitting something or a recovery resets the streak. Skimming close past a rock at speed without touching it adds 6%. Thread all fourteen gates in one lap for a mirage lap and a full tank. Wait for the countdown to show 1, then press accelerate for a rocket start; holding it earlier only revs the engine.

Release boost to recharge. Depleted boost rearms above 25% energy; loose sand disables boost. Automatic recovery returns you to the last earned gate and adds three seconds. After restarting or recovering, release drive controls, then press again. The results card shows your medal, rank, lap times and personal best.

Controls uses Tab or Up / Down arrows to navigate and Enter to select. Escape cancels a pending binding capture; otherwise Escape or F2 closes Controls. Close Details with Done or Escape. In fullscreen, Escape may exit fullscreen first. If the race is still paused after closing a panel, use your current Pause binding to resume.
